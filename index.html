About

I graduated with a civil engineering degree in 2024 and, while I'm still early in my career, I didn't want to wait for a job title to start building real work. This site is where I put that work: drawing sets, structural models, and site analysis I've built on my own time to get sharper with the tools the profession actually runs on.

Each project here started from a real-world scenario — an existing building, a public dataset, a site I could actually walk — worked through the way a working engineer would, from sketch to a finished, checkable deliverable. I'm looking for opportunities where I can keep doing this for real.
