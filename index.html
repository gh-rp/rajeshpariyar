About

I'm a civil engineer working across structural design, transportation, and water resources, with a habit of staying close to the site as much as the spreadsheet. My work sits between the calculation and the constructability review — the goal is always a structure that performs, and a drawing set a contractor can actually build from.

Before independent practice, I worked on municipal infrastructure and mid-rise structural design, coordinating with architects, surveyors, and geotechnical consultants from concept through construction administration. I care about tolerances, load paths, and reading a site correctly before the first line is drawn.
